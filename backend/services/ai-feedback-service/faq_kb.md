# AcadConnect — onboarding FAQ (help bot context)

## Roles
- **Student:** Can create/join **groups** (3–5 accepted members), create **projects** for their group (only the **group leader**), browse **recommendations**, send **mentorship requests** to faculty, and view **notifications** on the dashboard.
- **Faculty:** Can set **research areas** and **max mentee capacity** in profile. Receives **requests** from student groups, can **accept** or **reject**. When at **max capacity**, further accepts are blocked and pending requests may be auto-rejected.
- **Admin:** Can manage **student eligibility** (eligible / probation / ineligible), view **audit logs**, and see all **users** from the admin dashboard.

## Groups
- Create a group by inviting other students by email; each invitee **accepts** the invite to become an accepted member.
- A group typically needs **3–5 accepted members** before creating a project or applying for mentorship (enforced by the system).
- Only the **leader** can create a project for that group and submit mentorship requests for that project.

## Projects
- **Open:** posted by the student group; describes the idea.
- **In progress:** after a faculty **accepts** a mentorship request tied to that project.
- **Closed:** faculty or workflow may mark the project complete when work is done.

## Mentorship requests
- Submitted by the **group leader** for a chosen **open project** toward a **faculty mentor**, with a short **application snippet** (word limit applies).
- Duplicate requests to the same faculty for the same project are not allowed.
- One **accepted** mentor per project; other pending requests are then rejected.

## Eligibility
- **Admin** sets **eligibility_status** for students: **eligible**, **probation**, or **ineligible** (with optional reason stored in audit logs).
- Students can see their eligibility on the **Settings / Profile** page.

## Recommendations
- Faculty suggestions are based on **skills and interests** vs faculty **research areas**. You may need to sync/index faculty data depending on environment (see docs or ask an admin).

## AI project feedback
- Optional **AI feedback** helps review a project **title and description**; it is **not** a substitute for advisor approval.

## Getting help
If something is broken, missing, or unclear, contact your **course admin** or **system administrator**. Do not share passwords or API keys in chat.

PURPOSE:
This page explains the culminating experience requirement for SJSU graduate students, including thesis, project, comprehensive exam, creative work, doctoral project, dissertation, deadlines, verification forms, and continuous enrollment through 1290R.

OVERVIEW:
A culminating experience is required to complete a master’s or doctoral degree program at San José State University. It is the final requirement needed to complete the degree.

MAIN TOPICS:
1. Identify your culminating experience plan
2. Follow culminating experience deadlines
3. Maintain continuous enrollment

CULMINATING EXPERIENCE PLANS:

PLAN A — THESIS, MASTER’S DEGREE:
Plan A requires the student to select a thesis topic and receive approval from the Graduate Program Coordinator and/or the candidate’s committee. The student must successfully complete the thesis and produce it in bound form.

The thesis plan is overseen by the College of Graduate Studies.

Course enrollment:
Department 299.

Useful references:
- Thesis details, including forms, guidelines, and deadlines, are handled through the College of Graduate Studies.
- Thesis requirements are also listed in the SJSU Catalog.

PLAN B — PROJECT OR COMPREHENSIVE EXAM, MASTER’S DEGREE:
Plan B requires the student to complete a comprehensive examination, final paper, project, or a combination of these. Students should consult their Graduate Program Coordinator for guidance.

Course enrollment:
Department 298 for project, or another course set by the department.

Important note:
Students who complete the culminating experience through Plan B cannot list 299 with a passing grade on their SJSU transcript. If a student enrolled in 299 but is completing Plan B, they must withdraw from 299 or take a grade of NC.

PLAN C — CREATIVE WORK, MASTER’S DEGREE:
Plan C is used when the final requirement is completed through a special creative assignment. The student must submit a written report about the creative project, and the report must be cataloged.

Plan C follows a similar pattern to the thesis option and is overseen by the College of Graduate Studies.

Course enrollment:
Department 299.

Useful references:
- Creative Work details, including forms, guidelines, and deadlines, are handled through the College of Graduate Studies.
- Creative Work requirements are also listed in the SJSU Catalog.

PROJECT — DOCTORAL DEGREE:
California State University policy requires a doctoral project for the following programs:

Doctor of Nursing Practice, DNP:
The DNP doctoral project must be the written product of a systematic, rigorous, evidence-based effort focused on a significant nursing practice issue.

Doctor of Audiology, AuD:
Project proposals may be completed as projects developed under faculty guidance and approved by the doctoral project committee.

Course enrollment:
Department project course.

The doctoral project option is overseen by the College of Graduate Studies.

DISSERTATION — DOCTORAL DEGREE:
California State University policy requires a dissertation for Doctor of Education, EdD, programs.

The EdD dissertation must be the written product of systematic, rigorous research on a significant professional issue.

Course enrollment:
Department 599.

The dissertation option is overseen by the College of Graduate Studies.

Students completing a department project or comprehensive examination, Plan B, must successfully complete the experience by the listed degree conferral date at the end of the semester in which they intend to graduate.

Students should confirm the deadline on the Deadlines page for their anticipated graduation term. The date is found in the Important Dates and Timelines table.

Students should confirm the deadline through the College of Graduate Studies website or contact:
graduate-studies@sjsu.edu

VERIFICATION OF CULMINATING EXPERIENCE FORM:
The Graduate Admissions and Program Evaluations, GAPE, Office is notified that a student completed degree program requirements in two ways:

1. Submission of a Verification of Culminating Experience Form
2. Successful completion of a predetermined final culminating experience course

Purpose of the form:
The Verification of Culminating Experience Form tells the GAPE Office that the student has successfully satisfied all degree program requirements according to the designated department or school.

Who submits the form:
The Graduate Program Coordinator submits the form on behalf of the student at the end of the semester in which the student intends to graduate.

Student responsibility:
The student is responsible for confirming whether their program uses this form and making sure the form has been submitted to GAPE.

Important note:
The Verification of Culminating Experience Form is not accessible to students. It will not be accepted if submitted by a student. Only designated Graduate Program Coordinators have access and must submit it to GAPE on the student’s behalf.

For students whose program only requires a final culminating experience course, the student is responsible for successfully completing that predetermined course.

CONTINUOUS ENROLLMENT AND 1290R:

ENROLLMENT POLICY:
Continuous enrollment is required for all graduate students between the completion of regular courses and satisfactory completion of the culminating experience.

Enrollment must be maintained every Fall and Spring semester until degree completion.

Students may maintain enrollment by:
1. Registering for a 1-unit 1290R course
2. Registering for other courses

Departments may require repeated enrollment in a project, thesis, or comprehensive exam-preparation class. Students should consult their Graduate Program Coordinator for guidance.

1290R REGISTRATION:
Eligible graduate students are notified by the Registrar’s Office approximately six weeks before the semester starts.

Students are assigned a Special Session appointment for the 1290R course and may register online through MySJSU.

No permission code or add code is needed to register for 1290R.

1290R has no formal class meetings because it is supervisory in nature.

Purpose of 1290R:
1290R allows students to maintain active student status and continue using campus resources while completing their culminating experience.

Registration timing:
Students are encouraged to register as soon as the MySJSU appointment is available. Registration for 1290R must happen before the last day of course instruction for the given term.

International students:
International students are encouraged to add the course as soon as appointments are available and within 30 days in order to maintain immigration status.

RETROACTIVE 1290R REGISTRATION:
Continuous enrollment is a university graduation requirement.

Failure to register for required enrollment will result in a graduation hold until the 1290R unit is retroactively added for each Fall or Spring term in which enrollment was missing.

Late fees will apply.

To retroactively add 1290R, the student must complete the online Retroactive Requests for 1290R form and pay the associated tuition and fees.

IMPORTANT Q&A RULES FOR THE LLM:
- If asked whether a culminating experience is required, answer yes. It is required for completion of a master’s or doctoral degree program.
- If asked who submits the Verification of Culminating Experience Form, answer that the Graduate Program Coordinator submits it, not the student.
- If asked whether students can access or submit the Verification of Culminating Experience Form, answer no. It is not accessible to students and will not be accepted if submitted by a student.
- If asked about Plan A, explain that it is the thesis option for master’s students and uses department 299.
- If asked about Plan B, explain that it is the project or comprehensive exam option and usually uses department 298 for projects or another department-designated course.
- If asked whether Plan B students can have a passing grade in 299 on the transcript, answer no. They must withdraw from 299 or take NC if they enrolled in it.
- If asked about Plan C, explain that it is creative work for master’s students, requires a written report, and uses department 299.
- If asked about doctoral projects, explain that DNP and AuD programs require doctoral projects under CSU policy.
- If asked about dissertations, explain that EdD programs require dissertations under CSU policy and use department 599.
- If asked about thesis, dissertation, creative work, or doctoral project deadlines, direct students to the College of Graduate Studies and/or GAPE deadlines pages.
- If asked about project or comprehensive exam deadlines, answer that the requirement must be completed by the degree conferral date at the end of the intended graduation semester.
- If asked about continuous enrollment, answer that graduate students must remain enrolled every Fall and Spring until degree completion.
- If asked what 1290R is, answer that it is a 1-unit course used to maintain active enrollment while completing the culminating experience.
- If asked whether 1290R has formal class meetings, answer no.
- If asked whether 1290R requires an add code, answer no.
- If asked when to register for 1290R, answer before the last day of course instruction for the term.
- If asked about international students and 1290R, answer that they are encouraged to enroll as soon as the appointment is available and within 30 days to maintain immigration status.
- If asked what happens if a student fails to maintain continuous enrollment, answer that a graduation hold may be placed until missing 1290R units are retroactively added, and late fees apply.
